# Safety, Ethics & Scope

- Synthetic data only in this competition prototype.
- No diagnosis, prescription, dosage, or emergency-management instructions.
- Suggestions are bounded to self-management review, signal verification, and clinician discussion.
- Hypothetical alternatives are explicitly labeled hypothetical.
- Historical conclusions are separated from later evidence.
- Uncertainty is surfaced rather than hidden.
- Recovery is not declared from one measurement; sustained direction requires subsequent evidence.
- A future clinical deployment would require privacy/security controls, clinical validation, prospective evaluation, human oversight and regulatory assessment.
